# Vonk Elektra — Copy & Meta

**Tone:** "wonen en werken in comfort" — calm, plain-spoken, specific. Not salesy, not technical-showing-off. The existing English voice already has this; the job is to carry it into Dutch, not to flatten it.

**Brand pillars, and the words that carry them:**

| Pillar | Say it like this | Do not say |
|--------|------------------|------------|
| Skilled electricians | "vakmensen", "eigen monteurs", "getest voordat we weggaan" | "professioneel", "kwaliteit hoog in het vaandel" |
| Reliable and transparent | "duidelijk over de prijs", "geen verrassingen achteraf", "we bellen terug" | "transparant", "eerlijk" as a bare adjective |
| Competitive prices | "een scherpe prijs voor wat je echt nodig hebt", "geen upsell" | "goedkoopste", "laagste prijsgarantie" |

Three rules for this brand:

1. **Show, do not claim.** "Getest en uitgelegd voordat we weggaan" beats "kwaliteitswerk" every time. The existing copy already does this well — "we're there fast: evenings and weekends included" is a real commitment, not an adjective.
2. **Comfort is the outcome, electricity is the method.** Lead with what changes in the house. "Het hele jaar comfortabel, zonder gasrekening" sells better than "hybride warmtepomp met COP 4,5".
3. **No exclamation marks. No "Wij zijn dé...".** The voice is confident because it is specific, not because it is loud.

---

## The headline problem, and how to fix it without wrecking the voice

Every current H1 is a brand line with no search signal in it:

> "Living & working in complete comfort." · "The people behind the power." · "Every service, and the work behind it." · "Let's get it sorted."

These are good. Losing them would be a real cost. The fix is structural rather than editorial — the page has three slots above the fold, and right now the brand line is occupying the one that needs to work hardest:

| Slot | Current | Should be |
|------|---------|-----------|
| Eyebrow | "Electrician · Nijmegen & surroundings" | keep — it already does the local job |
| **H1** | brand line | **service + city** |
| Subheadline | plain description | **the brand line lives here** |

That single swap fixes the on-page targeting on all four pages without deleting a word worth keeping.

## Page-by-page

### Home — `/`

**Target:** elektricien Nijmegen · installateur Nijmegen · zonnepanelen Nijmegen

**Title** (58 chars)
`Elektricien in Nijmegen | Vonk Elektra`

Three options, pick per how much you want the services in the title:

- **A** — `Elektricien in Nijmegen | Vonk Elektra` — cleanest, brand-forward, best for repeat and referral traffic already searching your name.
- **B** — `Elektricien Nijmegen | Zonnepanelen, warmtepomp & storing` (58) — packs three services in. Better for cold search, weaker brand recall.
- **C** — `Vonk Elektra | Elektricien & duurzame installaties Nijmegen` (59) — the balance. **Recommended.**

**Meta description** (155)
`Elektricien in Nijmegen voor zonnepanelen, warmtepompen, laadpalen, airco en storingen. Duidelijke prijs, eigen monteurs, ook 's avonds bereikbaar.`

*Why:* leads with the query term, lists the services people search for, then two proof points. "Eigen monteurs" is the differentiator against the national resellers who subcontract.

**H1** — `Elektricien in Nijmegen voor wonen en werken in comfort.`

Keeps the brand spine ("wonen en werken in comfort") and puts the head term in front of it. This is the version to use.

Alternatives: `Uw elektricien in Nijmegen en omgeving.` (safest, dullest) · `Comfortabel wonen en werken, vakkundig geïnstalleerd.` (best voice, no local signal — only viable if the eyebrow carries Nijmegen hard).

**Subheadline** (the current hero paragraph, translated and tightened)

> Volledige gemoedsrust — u hoeft zich nooit af te vragen of alles goed werkt. Nemen we een klus aan, dan doen we hem goed. En gaat er iets stuk, dan staan we snel voor u klaar: ook 's avonds en in het weekend.

**Every service page follows the same skeleton:**

1. H1 (dienst + stad) → lead paragraph → CTA pair (offerte / bellen)
2. Wat u krijgt — the three bullets already in the `features` array, expanded to a sentence each
3. Hoe het gaat — the four-step process already on the services page: luisteren, prijs, uitvoeren, opleveren
4. Wat het kost — a range, and what moves it. This is the most-searched question and the one nobody answers. Answering it is a competitive advantage before it is an SEO one.
5. Recent werk — one project card, real photo
6. Veelgestelde vragen — three to five, marked up as `FAQPage`
7. Sluitende CTA — form plus phone number

**Title** (55) — `Over Vonk Elektra | Uw elektricien in Nijmegen`

**Meta** (152)
`Eén vast team voor elektra, zonnepanelen en storingen in Nijmegen. We luisteren eerst, zijn duidelijk over de prijs en leggen uit wat we gedaan hebben.`

**H1** — `De vakmensen achter Vonk Elektra`

**Story H2** — `Vakwerk waar u niet over na hoeft te denken.`

The existing story section is the strongest copy on the site — it names a real problem ("unclear quotes, jobs half-finished, no one picking up on a Friday night") and positions against it. Translate it as-is; do not soften it. That paragraph is doing more work than any headline on the site.

**Add for E-E-A-T:** names and faces of the team, years of experience, certifications and any branch membership, and the KvK number in visible text rather than only in the footer. For a trade business those are the trust signals search and customers both weigh.

## Open Graph and Twitter

Same values on every page, with the page's own title and description:

```html
<meta property="og:type"        content="website">
<meta property="og:site_name"   content="Vonk Elektra">
<meta property="og:locale"      content="nl_NL">
<meta property="og:title"       content="[page title]">
<meta property="og:description" content="[page description]">
<meta property="og:url"         content="[canonical URL]">
<meta property="og:image"       content="https://vonkelektra.nl/img/og-vonk-elektra.jpg">
<meta property="og:image:width"  content="1200">
<meta property="og:image:height" content="630">
<meta name="twitter:card"       content="summary_large_image">
```

The OG image needs making: 1200×630, logo on the `#f7f5f2` ground with the orange `#E67206` accent, and the phone number legible at thumbnail size. It is the first impression on every WhatsApp share — which, for a local installer working on referral, is a bigger channel than Twitter will ever be.

## What not to change

Three things in the current copy are better than what most installers ship. Translate them faithfully and leave them alone:

- **"We do it right, so you don't have to think about it."** The whole positioning in nine words.
- **The story paragraph on the About page.** Names the real problem and positions against it, without naming a competitor.
- **"Evenings and weekends included."** A concrete commitment where everyone else writes "24/7 service" and means a voicemail box.
