About Vonk Elektra

The people behind the power.

We're a Nijmegen electrician built on a simple idea: do the job right, be honest about the price, and be there fast when something fails. From solar to fault repair, one team looks after your home or business.

Our story

Craftsmanship you don't have to think about.

Vonk Elektra grew out of years of hands-on electrical work across Nijmegen and the surrounding area. We saw how often people were left guessing — unclear quotes, jobs half-finished, no one picking up when a fault appeared on a Friday night.

So we built the opposite. We listen before we propose, we're transparent about price, and we treat your home or business with respect. As the energy transition accelerated, we grew with it — adding solar, batteries, heat pumps, air conditioning and EV charging to the electrical work we've always done.

Today we're the single team you can call for comfort, sustainability and safety — from the first advice to the final test.
